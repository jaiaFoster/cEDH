## Organizer philosophy and 2HG rules sensitivity

- Sparse additive card pricing; most cards free. Approx 321 legal pointed identities was the prior audit result, **not a mandated final count**.
- Price cards by maximum realistic competitive strength in 2HG, with broad applicability rewarded, exceptional specialized combos appropriately priced. No team synergy surcharges, no partner-pair surcharges. Main-deck and command-zone prices distinct and alternatives. Partners sum their command-zone costs.
- Most combos should be controlled through high price on exceptionally powerful tutors, fast mana, protection, win enablers, and extra-team turns. **Only truly egregious combinations should be proposed for explicit prohibitions**; do not impose categorical infinite-combo/turn-7 bans without approval. Evaluate likelihood, windows of interaction and controller-specific legality, not theoretical best-case turn alone.
- Prefer heavily taxing cards exploiting shared 60-life and additional TEAM turns over blanket taxes on defensive one-turn protection. Symmetrical hate generally modest points; egregious asymmetric oppression can be separately flagged.
- Cyclonic Rift overload bounces teammate's permanents you don't control, whereas Silence affects both opponents but not teammate. Orim's Chant targets only ONE head: distinguish from Silence.
- Individual control, mana, life gain event, and card ownership still separate even though team turn and life are shared. Early quoted 2HG rules need verified citations and Oracle, avoid conflating each player/each opponent or paying/losing life.
- Extra-team turns: validate Final Fortune-family delayed loss, Expropriate team vote/turns, Mindslaver entire opponent team control.
- Commander-damage official document discrepancy and new October rulebreaker text from last audit must be pinned/identified, not handwaved.
- Heartless Hidetsugu main price still suggested not approved; **command zone** may be extremely threatening if genuinely eligible and 2HG legal, validate its commander eligibility and appropriate separate CZ price. Its unprotected activation at 60/60 can result in a draw; need asymmetry to win.
