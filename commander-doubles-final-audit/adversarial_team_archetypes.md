# Adversarial teams — illustrative cores and complete scoring fixtures

Each text list has100 legal cards but basic padding intentionally isolates the core. These are not optimized complete competitive decks. Turn lines are conditional feasible sequences, not goldfish medians or win probabilities. No paid API or gameplay simulator used.

## T01 — Actual zero-budget T2 drain

A: Grismold, the Dreadsower; B: Storrev, Devkarin Lich.

Roles/core A: Chain of Smog; B: Witherbloom Apprentice.

Old A/B/team: 0/0/0. Proposed: 3/0/3.

Demonstrated conditional turn 2: T1 each plays own Swamp. T2 A plays Forest (1B available); B plays Forest (BG available). B casts Apprentice; A casts Chain targeting B. B self-copies until 30 magecraft drains resolve.

Prerequisites: Both core spells drawn, two lands each, no opposing interference.

Disruption: Remove Apprentice, counter original/copy or essential triggers.

Classification: unpointed exceptional enabler; cross-team loop. Recommendation: Point Chain3 for repeatable copy access, leave ordinary payoff free; prohibit resource/win loops separately.
