# Proposed league legality policy — organizer approval required

PROPOSED ONLY. Standard Commander and Constructed2HG bans remain. No experimental unbans. Register BOTH decks and controller/zone-specific known win/lock lines before play. Reject repeatable unbounded resource/win/extra-combat engines, repeated extra turns or repeated control of opposing turns, and intentional mass land denial/participation locks from the submitted permitted-lines list; exact boundaries need organizer approval. Reject deliberately reliable pre-seventh-shared-turn deterministic wins, including combat loops, through construction review; merely waiting is not compliance. This is NOT temporary cannot-win/cannot-lose protection and does not alter Pact/Final Fortune/Ad Nauseam. No combo point surcharges. Ordinary finite synergies, interaction and team support remain allowed.

| Class | Proposed treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Allowed | Finite synergies, ordinary ramp/interaction, coordinated roles and teammate support without surcharge. |
| Point-regulated | Exceptional independently priced mana, tutors, protection and engines; actual zone determines fixed cost. |
| Restricted by rule | Registered repeatable win/resource/extra-combat engines; repeated extra turns/control of opponents; intentional severe participation locks and mass land denial. |
| Banned | Current official Commander/category/Constructed2HG bans; no experimental unbans. |

The shortest enforceable implementation is a published prohibited-line register with controller/state prerequisites, applied to BOTH submitted decks, plus an organizer appeals process. C01/C02/C07/C11/C12/C13 and T06-T08 are initial engine-review entries; C08-C10 are initial participation-lock entries. Add lines only by recorded ruling, not automatic name-union detection. A pool lock has ability/attack outs but still presents sustained participation denial.

Early-win intent review is necessarily less objective than card identity: test demonstrated lines and observed play frequency; do not call every perfect opening reliable. T02's two-Chancellor opener is an extreme possibility, while T01 is a cheap two-piece distributed line. This draft is not ready for automatic enforcement until organizer defines loop, lock, mass denial and appeals boundaries. No custom penalty or gameplay-loss rule has been activated by software.

Do not add a pre-turn7 cannot-lose shield or combat exemption: they distort Pact/Final Fortune/Ad Nauseam and admit Dualcaster/Najeela attacks. Count shared team turns; extra turns do not legitimize an otherwise prohibited engine. Official brackets are conversation guidance; numerical cap plus these custom restrictions is not literal officialB3 certification.
