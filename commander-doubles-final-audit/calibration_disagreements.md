Largest conceptual disagreements: Orim's Chant only shuts one head's spells while Silence shuts both; it nevertheless stays exceptional per organizer. Kicker prohibits all attacks that turn. Free Kenrith/Najeela/Inalla/Derevi/Yuriko retain guaranteed engines and access, despite previous5-8-point command prices. They are not proven safe; T06-T09 demonstrate risks. Skullclamp1/Pod2/Training1 are intentionally minor despite high specialized ceilings. Cyclonic Rift0 remains powerful with a spell/combo-focused teammate or useful ordinary bounce mode; bouncing a teammate reduces asymmetry but does not make it objectively weak. Tithe8 reflects an elite human judgment despite fewer opposing draw steps.

Canadian Highlander is a different format; its points and current Game Changer flags are preserved alongside prices in audited_point_schedule.csv. Sol Ring is exceptional here despite not being a Game Changer. Food Chain remains exceptional despite removal from Game Changers. GC is a curated experience category, not a quantitative strength scale. This system does not enforce the official3-Game-Changer B3 cap unless organizer separately adopts it; therefore call it a custom league with B3-like intent.
