# Breach the Bay 2 — Top 10 Tymna/Thrasios Card-by-Card Comparison

**Subject deck:** Creature Factory v165 (Moxfield updated 2026-08-23)  
**Event:** Breach the Bay 2, August 22–23, 2026 — 429 players, Top 64  
**Cohort:** The ten highest-finishing complete Tymna/Thrasios lists, not the ten best lists from a multi-event model.

## Method and limits

- `Lists` is inclusion among the ten-list cohort. `Top 64` is inclusion among the four cohort lists that made the event cut.
- `Δ Top–Rest` is Top-64 inclusion minus inclusion in the other six lists. It is directional only; four versus six observations is not inferential evidence.
- Card roles are functional labels for deck-building comparison, not exhaustive Oracle descriptions.
- The analysis is observational and informational. It does not recommend automatic cuts or treat correlation as causation.
- Raw event/deck data: [TopDeck.gg snapshot](https://github.com/jaiaFoster/cEDH/tree/claude/cedh-simulation-research-k5afxg/data/tournament_snapshots/topdeck). General T&T context: [cEDH Stats](https://cedhstats.org/commanders/thrasios-tymna/cards).

## Package findings

- **Shared core is large:** Creature Factory overlaps 67–76 cards with each cohort list. The dispute is a concentrated package choice, not the underlying commander shell.
- **The measurable trade:** Creature Factory has 33 creatures, 12 labeled engines, 15 interaction pieces, and two flash tools. Cohort averages are 28.4 creatures, 8.9 engines, 17.4 interaction pieces, and four flash tools. We are spending roughly three additional engine slots and four to five additional creature slots while giving up roughly two interaction slots and two flash slots.
- **Three universal omissions:** High Fae Trickster, Swan Song, and The Cabbage Merchant appeared in all ten lists but are absent from Creature Factory. They deserve direct slot-for-slot review, not automatic adoption.
- **Creature Factory’s structural divergence:** Birthing Ritual, Seedborn Muse, Sylvan Library, and Talion appeared in none of the ten. Birthing Pod appeared once and missed Top 64. These cards carry the burden of proving that the expanded permanent/conversion network outperforms the flash, interaction, and compact-win slots it displaces.
- **Compact Oracle split:** Thassa’s Oracle and Demonic Consultation appeared in 7/10, and Tainted Pact in 6/10. Creature Factory deliberately declines that compact win architecture, so raw card-count comparisons must account for the extra conversion slots required by its creature network.
- **Higher-finisher directional cluster:** Eternal Witness, Snap, and Delney each appeared in 3/4 Top-64 lists versus 3/6 of the remaining lists. Cloud of Faeries and Voice of Victory were also in 3/4 Top-64 lists, but were equally or more common below the cut. Only the first three show even a directional concentration, and none is proven by this sample.
