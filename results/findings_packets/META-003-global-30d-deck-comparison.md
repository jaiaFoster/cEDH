## Ranking method

- Eligible records required a structured 90+ card mainboard and a confirmed competitive event title.
- Result score = 6×winner + 3×top cut + 2.5×finish percentile + 0.3×points per game + 0.25×log2(field size). It selects a comparison cohort; it is not a player rating.
- Exact duplicate mainboards were deduplicated as deck designs, retaining the best result. No more than two selected designs may come from one event.
- Honorable mentions required a top cut/win, at least 35/98 overlap with Creature Factory, and at least three creature-network signals (Pod/Neoform/Survival/tutor/conversion pieces), then were diversified by commander pair and event.

## Limits

- Structured deck coverage was 3587/6352 (56.5%); field and conversion metrics are broader than the card-level sample.
- “Global” means every geography represented in the qualifying TopDeck pull, not a population-weighted sample of all cEDH played worldwide. Regions with heavier TopDeck adoption contribute more observations.
- Event-title filtering is conservative and reproducible. It can omit competitive events whose titles do not identify them as such, but prevents casual/bracket events from contaminating the pool.
- Card prevalence is observational. It does not establish causation or authorize automatic cuts.
