## Method and limits

- An event qualified when its TopDeck title explicitly identified cEDH/competitive EDH or an invitational qualifier; casual, bracket 2/3, precon, test, and budget-specific events were excluded.
- Regional boundaries are explicit and versionable: Arizona statewide plus Las Vegas and Henderson. Tucson is included as Arizona. Albuquerque and the rest of Nevada are excluded from this report.
- Field representation uses every standing. Card analysis uses only submitted structured deck objects, so zero-decklist Vegas weeklies affect prevalence but not card counts.
- Functional labels are reproducible Oracle-text heuristics plus small named-card sets; overlapping categories are intentional and counts are inventory measures, not equal-value ratings.
- Repeated pilots/event appearances remain separate metagame observations. Exact duplicate 98s are not silently treated as independent designs in the global ranking.
