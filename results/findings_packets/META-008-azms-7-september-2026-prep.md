## Version-14 deck audit against these threats

Construction: 98 mainboard, two commanders; strong creature-toolbox development, Kinnan/Badgermole Cub/Enduring Vitality/Delney scaling, Pod/Neoform/Evolution/Survival search, Remora/Rhystic/Lotho/Cabbage spell-pressure capture, Swan Song/Flusterstorm/Force/Fierce/Mindbreak stack backstop and Silence/Voice/Grand Abolisher protected turns. There is **no Oracle/Consultation/Pact compact win** in this 98. Recovery engines remain good, but first-turn speed and end-of-fight cash-out lag flash/compact lists; dedicated grave coverage is **Endurance plus Deathrite Shaman**, with modal artifact/enchantment answers relying on Boseiju/Skyturtle/Sowing Mycospawn and similar conditional lines. Source: [current 98](https://github.com/jaiaFoster/cEDH/blob/claude/cedh-simulation-research-k5afxg/data/deck_sources/moxfield/tymna-thrasios/current.json), [META-007 baseline](https://github.com/jaiaFoster/cEDH/blob/claude/cedh-simulation-research-k5afxg/results/findings_packets/META-007-creature-factory-archetype-fingerprint-and-matchup-spread.md).

The version-12 to version-14 content comparison changes exactly **one card**: `Formidable Speaker` → `Volatile Stormdrake`. [Speaker](https://gatherer.wizards.com/ECL/en-us/176/formidable-speaker) exchanges a discarded card for creature search; [Stormdrake](https://gatherer.wizards.com/search?searchTerm=Volatile_Stormdrake) exchanges with an opponent's creature, supplying four energy, so it can normally keep an exchanged creature of mana value **four or less**. That supplies a second steal-style response alongside Gilded Drake, notably for Kinnan/Tayam/Magda/Sisay and a creature hate piece. It does **not** remove Tayam's graveyard, an artifact engine, or a six-mana Godo reliably. Stealing a commander is disruption and potentially useful material, not a permanently solved matchup because it can be recast. The swap sacrifices one creature tutor and conversion redundancy. Given local Kinnan/Tayam pressure, the trade is defensible, not proven superior by an exact-98 outcome experiment.

Nearby comparison lists: [Dakota Cline's 8th-place Tucson T&T](https://moxfield.com/decks/AYB3Wun5aESqo54ErWjQLA) overlaps **74/98** current cards, uses `Flash Photography`, `High Fae Trickster`, `Deadly Rollick`, `Orim's Chant`, Training Grounds and Oracle/Consultation/Pact; [Ross Donaldson's 7th-place T&T](https://moxfield.com/decks/3S05d3DUuEW_IjoBtlG5yQ) overlaps **67/98** and is more compact/turbo-leaning. [Brice Quarton's winning Kinnan](https://moxfield.com/decks/wra9JpKGSEqI1HXtjzglYQ) includes Badgermole Cub, Enduring Vitality and Colossal Skyturtle, but is a different color identity (40-card overlap). These are useful *package counterfactuals*, not evidence that importing their Oracle or flash slots improves Creature Factory automatically.

## Matchup preparation and possible changes

| Table pressure | Version-14 plan | Residual risk / possible review |
|---|---|---|
| Kinnan + midrange | Stormdrake/Gilded Drake, creature conversion, Endurance as needed; let stack decks cover early instant wins while developing | Cast/activation may snowball before steal, and commander recast remains available. Know which hand actually answers the *resolved* commander. |
| Tayam, Magda, Sisay | Stormdrake often answers the commander temporarily; protect early engine, keep Boseiju/Skyturtle/Endurance lines live | Grave/treasure/artifact dependencies can persist after commandeering one creature. A cheap broad on-board answer is the strongest optional hedge. |
| Rograkh/Thrasios, Rograkh/Silas, Blue Farm | Stack backstop + Silence/Voice and Cabbage/Lotho profit from trading resources; cash out immediately after the exchange | Fast starts and compact postfight wins. Flash access, e.g. High Fae Trickster or a suitable copy line, is a review option, not a forced replacement. [High Fae Trickster](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/40/high-fae-trickster) gives your spells flash; [Flash Photography](https://scryfall.com/card/fic/463/flash-photography) is a copy sorcery that may be cast as though it had flash only if it targets your permanent. |
| Kinnan plus Blue Farm; Tayam plus Blue Farm | Invite Blue Farm to fight the dangerous spell, contribute the missing answer when you have it, reserve a protected conversion turn | A Blue Farm player is **not** an automatic matchup fix: generic T&T PPG was 1.233 in 180 prior permanent-engine-plus-blue-stack pods vs 1.289 across 717 known pods. Pilot/seat/list confounding prevents applying that number to this exact 98. [META-007](https://github.com/jaiaFoster/cEDH/blob/claude/cedh-simulation-research-k5afxg/results/findings_packets/META-007-creature-factory-archetype-fingerprint-and-matchup-spread.md). |

**Optional candidate, only if testing reveals an expendable slot:** a cheap permanent removal spell (for instance Abrupt Decay for mana value ≤3, or a broader removal spell for artifacts/enchantments); evaluate against keeping creature density, colors, and fast-mana/stack backstop. `High Fae Trickster` is a second candidate if the main failure is being a full turn behind at postfight conversion. Neither has a documented exact-list performance gain, and no specific current card earns a defensible cut from the available sample. Keep the core engine intact. The deck-builder, not this meta agent, should decide a swap.
