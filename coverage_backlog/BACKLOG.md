## Ranking (frequency × impact, per README.md's method)

As of 2026-08-12, ranked by what actually blocks the most downstream work,
not by ID order:

1. **`SIM-0007` — highest impact, highest frequency.** Blocks Phase 4 (combo
   dependency graph), Phase 26 (combo accessibility), and any future
   simulation's ability to use a single deterministic transition beyond
   `INT-0002` — 13 of 14 discovered interactions are still gated. Frequency
   is effectively "every one of these 13 lines, every time the deck's
   combo-accessibility is analyzed" — this is the single highest-leverage
   remaining item. Sub-ranked internally by how validate-able each is
   without new tooling: `INT-0007..0014` (rules-only, no accumulating-
   resource ambiguity) are cheaper to close than `INT-0001`/`0004`/`0005`/
   `0006` (need careful mana-accounting or engine reproduction to resolve
   the break-even-vs-infinite question responsibly).
2. **`SIM-0001` — moderate impact, low frequency, but foundational.** Every
   downstream deck-backed artifact (Gate 1 closure, gold games, eventual
   simulation) inherits whatever risk this carries. Frequency is low (one
   check, not recurring) but the *consequence* of being wrong is systemic
   — the entire frozen deck hash, every card record, and every future
   simulation config all key off `tymna-thrasios-treefarm-v1`. Ranked above
   `SIM-0004` despite lower urgency because a wrong subject deck silently
   invalidates everything else, whereas a missing Energy mechanic only
   affects one card's fidelity.
3. **`SIM-0004` — low impact, lowest frequency.** Affects exactly one
   card's ETB ability (Volatile Stormdrake) and only matters if/when a
   native (non-Forge) rules tracker is built — moot entirely if the
   eventual `sim/rules_engine/` adapter wraps XMage or Forge directly
   (both already implement Energy natively, confirmed for Forge). Lowest
   priority of the three open items; revisit only once the native-vs-
   engine-wrapper architecture decision for `sim/rules_engine/` is made.
