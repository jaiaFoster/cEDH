About Commander Spellbook
The Commander Spellbook project is a search engine for Commander/EDH combos and that makes them easily available across all modern digital platforms. This community driven project is used to power
EDHREC's Combo Feature
.
The source code for the website and the backend server are
completely free and open source under the MIT license
.
Interested in the development of Commander Spellbook? Head over to
our Discord server
and invoke our @Artificers.
