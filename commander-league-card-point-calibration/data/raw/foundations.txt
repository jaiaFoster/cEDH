Foundations Commander Decklists
Announcements
Sep 18, 2026
Jubilee Finnegan
Join in the fun of Commander,
Magic
's most popular multiplayer format, with
Foundations
Commander decks! Releasing alongside
Reality Fracture
, these ready-to-play decks give you everything you need to start playing and loving Commander, including key cards, tokens, and a traditional foil borderless commander to lead the charge!

Foundations
Commander Deck
These decks are designed to introduce the Commander format to new players. Each monocolor deck employs a simple, straightforward, and exciting strategy like artifacts, big creatures, or Dragons! No matter how you like to play
Magic
, one of these decks is sure to spark your love for Commander. Longtime players can also look forward to playing with or against these decks, as each one puts the fun of multiplayer
Magic
at the forefront. Each
Foundations
Commander deck contains the following:
1 Ready-to-play 100-card Commander deck
1 Traditional foil face commander with borderless art
99 Non-foil cards
10 Non-foil double-sided tokens
1 Reference card
1 Deck box
You can find each decklist below and browse all the cards from
Foundations
Commander decks and
Reality Fracture
in the
Reality Fracture
Card Image Gallery
