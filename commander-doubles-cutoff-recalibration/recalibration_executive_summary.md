# Commander Doubles full-list recalibration — season0-proposed

**Recommendation: impact cutoff >77.5, nonlinear integer curve, 320 legal pointed identities (278 main-deck prices, 64 command-zone options), with an explicit location/authority exception ledger.** This replaces the earlier321-identity proposal only if approved. It is not activated. The cutoff is a sparsity policy; no natural statistical break or win-probability calibration was demonstrated.

The coherent philosophy remains fixed additive prices with separate main/CZ alternatives and no synergy surcharge. Every 9,610 inherited candidate route, top1000 record and 3,510 command-zone candidates is reconsidered for price authority, legal identity, functional nomination and cutoff. That is not a claim of 9,610 new expert strength assessments. Original impact scores are immutable, all 50 qualitative anchors preserved, all later explicit numerical corrections/ranges applied separately. The prior backend's tier→integer interval enforcement is removed because the organizer never approved those hard bands. Around97% of precon construction points and91% of cEDH-labelled points come from fixed calibration/family exceptions (exact shares in exception_dependence.csv), so changing a cutoff is not independent validation of the schedule.

## Rules and functional-equivalent findings

805.8 makes extra turns/skip steps/team control material. Necro skips both heads' draw step; Expropriate two friendly time votes yield two team turns; Savor skips both untaps; Mindslaver controls whole opposing turn. Shared60 amplifies life engines but individual resources stay separate. Fixed exception prices dominate cohort totals (see exception_dependence.csv); apparent statistical separation does not independently validate those judgments. Sorin sets enemy pool10; Reservoir50 is not initial lethal. Hidetsugu/Fraying need survival asymmetry or can draw the game. Breena life comparison fails against equal opposing shared pool. Silence targets both opponents; Chant only one. Rift overload hits teammate permanents.

True premium equivalents reviewed: Time Warp/Manipulation/Capture6 and Final Fortune/Last Chance/Oath7 aligned; Ouphe/Null Rod/Stony2 aligned. ESG6/SSG5/Chancellor5 and Swords1 versus proposed free removal remain documented judgment differences. No model claim of measured one-point superiority. Spellbook-style split RIP/Helm works; split Bond/Blood, Oracle/Consultation, Kinnan/Basalt and Tivit/Sieve fail; copying/controller/rules prerequisites cached and checked separately.

## Residual low-point risks and budget

Kenrith/Najeela/Inalla/Derevi/Yuriko stay free in CZ under human anchors despite high access assessments. Double-Yuriko's demonstrated T3 stacked-Draco attack, Najeela supported combats (basic-only fixture corrected T5→T6 for GG/WUBRG feasibility), Chain/Apprentice, and Dualcaster/Twinflame remain cheap conditional cores. High prices on premium acceleration/tutors/protection buy consistency, but price alone does not remove every fast line. All 21 constructed fixture teams are legal; exact points and prerequisites appear in adversarial_retest.md. No claim of consistent opening rates or optimized100-card construction.

Test **60 per-player reference /120 team /72 individual maximum, up to12 transfer** during recorded Season0. The budget has been stress-tested, not certified fair. No blanket infinite-combo or turn-seven ban is imposed. Official banned cards remain illegal; experimental prices confer no permission.
