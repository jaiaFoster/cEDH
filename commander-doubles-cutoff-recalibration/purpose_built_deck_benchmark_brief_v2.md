# Commander Doubles — Archetype-first benchmark roster v2

Status: PLANNING ONLY; no completed decks, point approvals, unbans or restrictions activated. Supersedes roster suggestions in `purpose_built_deck_benchmark_brief.md`. This is an amendment, not a claim that decks were constructed. Source: organizer feedback, October 20 2026.

## Critical fixes to v1
1. **Every player has their own complete Commander configuration.** In a two-player team, "Tymna + Thrasios" formerly named two separate heads' commanders but discarded the substantial Partner mechanic. Build both partners for any head where eligible and strategically worthwhile; total both command-zone point prices and correctly use 98 cards in that player's library when they have two commanders. Don't force Partner on standalone commanders such as Najeela, Yuriko or K'rrik.
2. **Give all 16 decks a real named archetype, explicit win architecture and independently useful game plan**, borrowing vocabulary from actual Legacy, Modern, Vintage and cEDH archetypes. Analogy is about strategy, NOT evidence the Commander list is a known competitive deck.
3. **Team synergy is intentional, but the two decks should not be identical or fail to a single answer by default.** No artificial synergy-point surcharge. No imagined shared mana, control, graveyard or library.
4. **Correct Najeela rules**: Najeela's activated ability untaps ALL attacking creatures and creates an additional shared-team combat phase; can enable teammate attacks. Warrior trigger is any attacking Warrior and its controller creates token. Verify with up-to-date Oracle.
5. **Primeval Titan remains officially banned in Commander.** A Lands/Primeval Titan benchmark is a separate explicitly labeled hypothetical experimental-unban scenario and must NOT be included as legal baseline unless organizers formally enact that unban.

## Outputs and reviewer experience
- Minimum 16 legal complete Commander decks with actual 100-card lists, identities, CZ partners and main cards, scoring from recalibrated schedule plus the latest 17 direct organizer numerical overrides (do NOT use outdated 4809836 as sole snapshot).
- Per-head deck title, Legacy/cEDH ancestor, primary plan, conversion package, interaction type, 2HG synergy and shared-vulnerability assessment.
- Per-team Moxfield imports, 60/120/72 budget validation, Oracle/ban/commander validation, mana-role profile and mulligan examples. Do not pretend legality verified unless validation actually run.
- Written and spoken-friendly 100–150 word briefing for each TEAM: describe A deck, B deck, how they interact, how they win and why it may be over/under target.
- User explicitly wants to judge **whether these decks feel like intended Bracket-3-adjacent Commander** before validating point cutoff/budget. Don't claim external archetype labels or theoretical turn clocks prove measured balance.
