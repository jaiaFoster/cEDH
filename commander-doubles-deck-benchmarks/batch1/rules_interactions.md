## A01 — Thassa's Oracle, Demonic Consultation, Tainted Pact

Required: Oracle and library-exile effect belong to A1. Oracle ETB on stack, own UUB and protection available. For Pact all own main-deck names unique.

A1 casts Oracle then Consultation naming absent legal card Forest to exile own library; resolving Oracle can win friendly team.

Caution: A1 Oracle plus A2 empty library is false. Oracle removal alone may not beat already-empty library; counter trigger, change library or prevent win. Pact/Consultation are not targeted spells for Misdirection.

CR 109.5, 603.3a, 810.5, 810.8a.

## A02 — Devoted Druid, Swift Reconfiguration, Walking Ballista

Required: A2 owns/controls Druid and Aura, has own Thrasios access and eventual Ballista.

Uncrewed Druid is noncreature Vehicle retaining abilities. Repeated untap counters do not cause creature toughness SBA; make arbitrary G. Noncreature tap ability does not have summoning sickness.

Caution: Do not crew it. Removal of Aura can make the heavily countered creature die. Same logic does not let teammate spend its mana.

CR 109.5, 302.6, 704.5, 810.5.

CR 109.5, 810.5.

## B01 — Najeela, the Blade-Blossom, Derevi, Empyrial Tactician

Required: B1 Najeela, B2 Derevi. At least five B2 creature-to-player combat damage events (or fewer with higher-output sources) untap B1 appropriate mana sources.

B2 chooses B1 lands as Derevi targets. B1 taps own sources and pays WUBRG during combat before mana empties; extra combat phase is shared and all currently attacking creatures untap.

Caution: B2 cannot pay Najeela cost with B2 mana. Extra phase is not extra turn/main. Activating on opponents combat adds combat to that opposing team turn; normally detrimental.

CR 109.5, 805.8, 805.10, 810.5, 106.4.

## B02 — Najeela, the Blade-Blossom, Universal Automaton, Stadium Headliner

Caution: B1 may decline enemy Warrior triggers. Tokens are white; they are not automatically five-color permanents for Bloom Tender.

## B03 — Nature's Will, Bear Umbra, Sword of Feast and Famine, Druids' Repository

## C02 — Yuriko, the Tiger's Shadow, Narset, Parter of Veils, Notion Thief, Drannith Magistrate, Cursed Totem, Pithing Needle

Required: Commander ninjutsu from own CZ, put-top-card trigger and relevant opposing hate.

Yuriko puts revealed card in hand rather than draws; ordinary draw denial does not stop that trigger. Commander ninjutsu is activated ability, not casting, so Drannith/cast tax do not block it. Cursed Totem creature-permanent restriction does not ban a creature card CZ ability.

Caution: Needle named Yuriko applies to source abilities across zones and can stop both same-named commanders ninjutsu. Normal commander casting remains subject to Drannith/tax, and burning still requires actual own Ninjas connecting.

## R01 — Otawara, Soaring City, Boseiju, Who Endures, Kutzil, Malamet Exemplar, Teferi, Time Raveler

Required: Channel owner has correct own hand/colored mana; land not already played, and eligible target.

Channels are activated abilities and can answer relevant permanents through spell-only restrictions. Boseiju can hit opposing artifact/enchantment/nonbasic land; Otawara can hit an eligible nonland permanent.

Caution: One already-played land is not a channel card in hand. Boseiju does not target every creature; Otawara does not bounce every land.

CR 109.5, 602, 805.5.

## R02 — Torpor Orb, Cursed Totem, Collector Ouphe, Devoted Druid, Swift Reconfiguration, Walking Ballista

Required: Relevant hate is on battlefield; do not claim universal hate coverage.

Orb stops ETB-trigger combos/Oracle but not replacement counters on cast Ballista or combat Yuriko triggers. Totem stops creature activated abilities, while uncrewed Swift Druid is noncreature; conversion may still need removing Totem. Ouphe stops artifact activations but not casting/bouncing Crypt in Caller loop.

Caution: Layered hate requires an actual available legal answer and its own mana/controller. Infinite G alone does not certify a win through every hate piece.

CR 109.2, 109.5, 614, 704.5.

Caution: Bruse only targets own creature; its life gain affects shared pool but the ability is not a blanket teammate creature buff.
