# Archetype lineage and source limitations

Twelve complete archived registrations, two per head, plus six public Moxfield lists were inspected. Ordinary Commander standings do not validate these new 2HG adaptations. Pilot names are not asserted original designers. `sources.csv` records exact event/date/standing/field/player locator, publication author and snapshot hashes. A TopDeck event registration URL plus the player locator identifies the list; no fabricated direct individual permalink is supplied. Public v3 Moxfield list metadata returned200; full primer prose was not obtained (loading page, tested primer endpoint404). These are inspected decklists and original analysis, not claims to have read inaccessible primers. Only factual card/count/author data is committed; raw Moxfield responses remain local and ignored.

## A1 — Blue Farm — Shared-Turn World Police

Retain the Blue Farm identity: Tymna/Kraum, cheap evasive value bodies, Remora/Sentinel, reactive stack control and Oracle/Consultation/Pact. Primary registration T1 contains these compact wins and explosive ritual/Breach/fast-artifact packages. Drop the latter package rather than paying for both complete engines. No Mana Vault, Sol Ring, premium Moxen, Silence, or Grand Abolisher is smuggled in for free. Replace much of that acceleration/protection with ordinary talismans, independent land development, targeted discard, Teferi, Tidal Barracuda, Abeyance and three-point Chant. Vault, Symmetry and BBB-requiring Beseech are slower consistency tools, not direct equivalents to one-mana tutors. Farm bodies and black discard give the head a meaningful role while Dawnwaker develops. Double-faced Fable supplies an engine/body rather than a random zero-point slot. A1 keeps Spell Queller, Aven Mindcensor and Faerie Macabre to cover distinct interaction axes.

## A2 — Dawnwaker — Dork/Untap Conversion

Preserve Thrasios plus Bruse, mana creatures, untap/reduction engines and creature-tutor chains. The old DJCor/PKCore list is historical, not a current legality template. Modern registrations confirm the commander pairing exists but do not establish this adaptation’s strength: one selected 54-player registration finished 47th. Retain Kinnan, Bloom Tender, Devoted Druid, Basalt, Zirda, Thrasios and Pod/toolbox structure; add Floodcaller/Helix/Crypt as an actual redundant own-controller mana route and Badgermole Cub/Formidable Speaker as relevant current creature engines. Remove extra premium free interaction to fund the conversion package. Keep Fierce, Chant and ordinary counters, including independent land-channel answers. Reclamation Sage is reachable by green creature tutors with generated G; Loran alone is not. Bruse is not claimed to pump Tymna across controllers. Seedborn Muse generates resources on the opposing shared untap, not separate untaps for both opposing heads.

## B1 — Najeela — Five-Color Warrior Tempo

The Warrior registration foundation contains actual Najeela pressure engines, including Ainok Strike Leader, Gornog, Stadium Headliner and Samut. Preserve those characteristics instead of reducing the deck to generic five-color good stuff. Nature’s Will, Bear Umbra, Sword and Repository are different combat-resource routes; own Derevi is redundancy if the ally commander is unavailable. Keep the compact Dualcaster/Twinflame backup with Goblin Bombardment as a noncombat conversion option. Drop premium fast artifacts and most priced auxiliary free spells. Demonic Tutor is worth fifteen here because it finds the specific converter/protection needed without forcing blue cards into a green/Warrior shell. Force of Will was rejected after a pitch audit found only eleven other blue printed cards in that draft. Keep Fierce, Veil, discard, two-color interaction and permanent spell restrictions; the other head carries Force. The five-color manabase is expensive in money but mostly free in construction points, a real schedule advantage.

## B2 — Derevi — Bant Pod and Untap Toolbox

Recent Derevi registrations with recorded standing1 supply Pod/Preston/Guardian, dorks, untaps and proactive creature interaction. Preserve a full independent Pod conversion rather than only lending Derevi to Najeela. Add Universal Automaton as a one-mana Warrior that can receive a Najeela token under B2 control and become a Pod seed. Own Cradle, Kinnan/Cub, Elder, Emiel, Finale/Ballista and Druid/Swift are useful alternatives. Drop Training Grounds after the role audit: it cannot reduce the card’s command-zone ability and this head has no Thrasios. Keep the tutorable Biomancer’s Familiar for actual creature abilities; replace Grounds with Glen Elendra Archmage, whose U-funded persist counter loop works with own Vizier. Torpor Orb attacks the Pod/ETB plan but not Najeela’s ordinary combat engine. We have not copied a source’s greedy 2HG-unverified stax package; own team engines remain functional under its selected hate.

## C1 — Yuriko — Ninja Tempo / Delver

The tempo sources use a high density of cheap evasive bodies, Ninjas, stack answers and Oracle/Consultation. Retain that identity with Force, Flusterstorm, Shoal, Misdirection, Bowmasters and unpointed conditional counters/discard. No pooled commander triggers or shared draws are assumed. One deliberate sixteen-mana Draco is retained despite being poor to cast: it is a deterministic team Symmetry target, not a general justification for stuffing tempo with dead cards. Scroll Rack/Brainstorm can return a drawn Draco, but require actual costs and timing. Additional independent Time Warp can yield another shared attack turn. The cited collaborative Moxfield list explicitly says OUTDATED; recent tournament registrations are the main deck-level evidence. Expensive extra free answers were cut before evasive density or basic Oracle conversion.

## C2 — Yuriko — Big-Flip Topdeck Combo

The distinct combo foundation includes Stian’s Draco/Shadow/Rack/Temporal Mastery registration and Sebbeth’s Doomsday/Nanogene registration. Preserve a larger deliberate high-mana package, top manipulation, own-copy multipliers and extra turns. This is not the same 99 relabeled. Doomsday supports two own Yuriko triggers revealing Draco then Blinkmoth Infusion; it does not empty the ally Oracle player’s library. Floodcaller enables Nanogene after blockers rather than incorrectly relying on fear against opponents that have become black Yuriko copies. Vampiric and Mystical survive because top access matters disproportionately; C1 supplies Force/Fluster. Keep playable delve, split and pitch spells alongside Draco/Shadow, rather than arbitrary expensive blanks. The public JacobParis Moxfield response contains only 94 cards at retrieval; it is conceptual lineage, not a complete validated current list. Mystic Retrieval was removed from our draft because its red flashback makes it illegal in UB.
