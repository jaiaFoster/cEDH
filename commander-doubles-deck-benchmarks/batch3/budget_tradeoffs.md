# Budget choices

G is49/71 with11 transferred to G2. G1 keeps Ritual9 for speed, Opposition Agent8 for enemy tutors and Imperial Seal8 for finding its defining conversions; Sorin/Peer5 each are expensive in mana but unusually decisive with shared life. G2 keeps Demonic15 for any-card access, Enlightened9 for Test/Blood, Test7 for starting60 thresholds and Remora6 for maintaining interaction. Mana Drain7 was cut for Negate0: reserving colored protection and finishing within72 matters more than banking colorless. The78-point G2 draft violated the individual maximum; replacing the7-point counter reduced it to71 and allowed G1's8-point Seal upgrade, producing49/71.

H is69/51 with9 transferred to H1. Its11-point Partner pair, Force14 and Oracle/Consultation20 already consume45. Remora6 and Sentinel5 are retained for card economy. Wishclaw4, Spellseeker4, Bowmasters3 and Dig1 were removed from H1, and a3-point Swan became existing cheap counters. H2 instead pays Worldly6 for creature selection; Recruiter4, Call4, Speaker1 and Wargate2 retain toolbox identity. Cradle15, premium free counters, Enlightened9 and fetch/dual5 were excluded to keep Aluren and supporting redundancy. This is not a guarantee those omissions cost no performance.

I is54/66 with6 transferred to I2. Gitrog keeps Ritual9, Entomb8, Worldly6 and Finale9 because finding or deploying the correct engine is important; Boseiju6 remains a flexible hate answer. I2 retains Force14, Teferi7, Remora6 and Otawara6 to protect both plans. It drops source Sol Ring15 and artifact tutors8–9 rather than its actual Stoneblade package. Rain of Filth1 became Nature's Lore0 in I1 to remove the final team overage and add durable mana rather than sacrificing lands for a one-shot gain. No points were refunded for team synergy.

All six use basic/conditional fixing rather than original fetches/duals at5. This is a real mana constraint, not proof those sources are equivalent. Shocks2 were weighed against actual colors and spell priorities; current lists choose ordinary basics/checks/fastlands and Signets. MDFCs have no blanket channel tax: Malakir Rebirth0 remains a disclosed review risk, not approved exact0. No weak card was added just to consume the final point.
