# Batch3 team plans — season0-proposed

## G — Denial plus enchantment control
G1 establishes black mana, Sheoldred, discard and renewable fodder. G2 places colored rocks, finds Test/Felidar or assembles its own enchantment drain loop, and keeps stack interaction. G1 can convert Bloodletter+Pox, Sorin+Bloodletter, or Peer+Sheoldred into decisive life loss. G2 Wound Reflection can finish G1's Sorin or Pox without shared control. G2's own Exquisite/Bond/Tenacity and G1's own Blood/Vito packages are legitimate independent alternatives; splitting these life-event engines is not.

The lock plan is deliberately adversarial. Contamination/Ophiomancer can deny both enemies while Oloro gains life from the command zone. It also turns G2 lands into black-only sources: require its nonland colored rocks or a deployed win before committing. Bottomless Pit, Necrogen Mists, Liliana, Smallpox, Death Cloud and Pox cost G2 cards or permanents too. Deploy durable enchantments and rocks first, or choose the quick threshold plan instead. G2 Replenish recovers only its own enchantments. Pox is not a free asymmetrical wipe.

Against fast spell combo, use Duress/Thoughtseize on the more dangerous head and G2 Veto/ordinary counters on the stack. A single discard spell cannot clear two hands. Against creature decks, black edicts/Deluge and G2 Verdict trade cards but threaten your own engines. Graveyard hate attacks drain/reanimation but not an already-deployed Test. Opposing Drannith limits battlefield commanders but not Oloro's command-zone trigger. Life-total locks, counters, enchantment removal and attack pressure challenge Test. This pair is mana-hungry and has fewer free counters than H/I; no win percentages claimed.

## H — Two independent finishers plus global creature permission
H1's changelings/Pirates/evasive threats generate Malcolm Treasure, Tymna cards and combat pressure. H2's mana dorks develop Chulane, with Aluren, Earthcraft, Alarm, bounce creatures and creature tutors supplying overlapping engines. H1 can win through its own Oracle/Consultation; H2 can draw toward its own Lab Maniac; H1 Acererak plus H2 Aluren is a separate cross-controller win requiring neither commander. Chulane never draws for teammate casts.

Aluren is global: all four players may flash eligible creatures. Enemy Opposition Agent, Bowmasters, Faerie Mastermind and combo creatures may exploit that window. Tidal Barracuda is a separately paid4-mana way to suppress enemy casting during the friendly turn; it is not itself Aluren-eligible. Chant/Abeyance restrict one enemy, leaving the other to interact. Speaker/Earthcraft can explicitly target a teammate's land, while Curio and ordinary bounce engines refer to their own controller's permanents.

Countering Aluren, removing Acererak at the right time, Torpor Orb/Hushbringer, Rule of Law, Drannith and creature ability suppression attack different routes. A deployed Lab Maniac needs protection from removal before the final draw. Oracle's trigger is different and may beat simple creature removal. Do not cast H1 Oracle into a resolved hostile draw effect or an ETB suppressor. H1 deliberately omits anti-creature stax and global draw denial that would shut off H2. H1 keeps14-point Force despite the squeeze because protecting a team-wide win is its role. Evasive creatures do not guarantee hits through two defending boards.

## I — Lands plus Stoneblade control
I1 uses Dakmor outlets, Loam, sacrifice/recursion and land tutors for draw/mana. It has a fully specified non-random Woodland/Analyst/Spelunking/Resources loop after removing Gitrog, as well as draw-dependent Dakmor routes and Dark Depths pressure. I2 deploys Stoneforge threats and Shorikai Pilots while keeping blue interaction; Scepter/Reversal or Basalt/Rings plus Ballista gives an independent own-controller artifact route.

The choice of Shorikai is intentional: its actual tournament shells provide Azorius filtering, artifact mana and stack protection, while its Pilot bodies carry Equipment. It is eligible under current903.3 and remains free in the command zone. The Stoneblade package changes the source engine materially, so this exact hybrid is newly designed, not an attributed tournament winner.

Do not import source RIP/Cage/Totem/Humility just because they are strong Shorikai cards. They sabotage Gitrog. Lantern's enemy-only graveyard exile is appropriate. Opposing RIP/Endurance/Surgical can hurt I1; I2's independent non-graveyard artifact and Equipment routes remain useful. Artifact hate attacks I2 but not Loam/Depths. Combat blockers, edicts and exile answer Marit Lage/Kaldra; a20/20 is not a60-life kill. Sword untaps I2 lands only, and Equip cannot attach to I1's creatures. Never borrow I1 mana for I2's loop.

H dungeon accounting: using fully resolved Lost Mine rooms requires119 casts and29 Temple draws to reach30 Dark Pools. At60 enemy shared life, retain>=29 library cards and do not assume unlimited safe draws. This qualification does not remove theT3 example, but matters late in a long game.
