## Card/rules clarifications and remaining audit
- **Solitary Confinement** (currently G2 0): enchantment {2}{W}; skip own draw step; at beginning of upkeep sacrifice unless discard a card; controller has shroud and all damage to controller prevented. In 2HG, **does not give teammate shroud or prevent damage assigned to teammate**, so opponents can still reduce shared life via the teammate (2HG CR 810.5, 810.9; official WotC 2HG overview). Does not protect against loss of life effects, only damage. With independent draw engines its drawback can be mitigated.
- **Bloodthirsty Conqueror** (G1 0) duplicates Exquisite Blood's relevant life-loss-to-gain trigger, combines with Vito; **Enduring Tenacity** (G2 0) duplicates Sanguine Bond's relevant gain-to-loss trigger, combines with Exquisite Blood. Both remain **zero until the organizer prices them explicitly**; flag these functional-substitute loopholes for next pass (Sources: Wizards FDN release notes, Wizards DSK release notes).
- The organizer judged the G team not catastrophically overpowered, prioritizing combination tax rather than blanket Pox-effect tax; this is distinct from its revised points.
- All changes apply main-deck card prices consistently to duplicates/copies across the six batches and future lists. Preserve pending flags vs exact decisions in future acceptance ledger.
