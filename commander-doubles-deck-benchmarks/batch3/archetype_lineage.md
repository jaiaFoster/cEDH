# Attributed foundations and original adaptations

All twelve complete source registrations are frozen in research/foundations.json with raw snapshot checksums, event/player locators and exact card inventories. Standings are those of the source events, not these adaptations. Every head has two list foundations, but their empirical strength differs substantially.

G1: the archived Sheoldred pilots include a17/18 event entry and a24/40 entry, not demonstrated elite results. Their draw-punishment, Peer, black ritual and life-drain packages supply concrete inventories. Reid Duke's2012 Pox article supplies the established Pox/Smallpox/Liliana/Hymn attrition lineage. Adding Sorin, shared-life Bloodletter/Pox conversion and Oloro support is original2HG engineering. Do not claim that Sheoldred Pox is a tournament-proven winning cEDH archetype.

G2: Albert's12/25 registration is explicitly a casual-labelled event; Jeff Flynn's20/31 cEDH-labelled entry went0-4. They are actual source lists, not strong performance evidence. Mitrian's published Oloro primer supports enchantment, lifegain and alternate-win identity. Mesa Enchantress, enchantment copies, Rector, Replenish, Test and an independent Blood payoff package make this an Esper enchantment-control deck, not green Enchantress translated illegally. Lower-powered creature payloads in source lists became efficient counters and card selection.

H1: Pedro Vidaurri and Ty Ohio have actual first-place registrations in38- and35-player events. Their Tymna/Malcolm compact-win/interaction packages are the strongest source pedigree in this batch. The budget removes ubiquitous expensive mana/tutors and preserves Force/Oracle/Consultation plus evasive Pirates. Acererak is a new cross-team role, not a card attributed to either source list.

H2: David Freijo's5/22 and Duncan McClellan's6/22 Chulane lists both actually contain Aluren. Dork acceleration, Curio/Drake, Alarm/Earthcraft and creature toolbox packages are retained in spirit and exact-card comparisons. Black Cavern Harpy and Acererak are illegal in Bant and absent from H2; Acererak instead belongs to H1. Stefan Stasiak's primary Legacy interview informs the complementary value/combo architecture without claiming his Legacy results for Chulane.

I1: Jason Scott's1/18 and Toni Romero's2/44 Gitrog registrations provide complementary Dakmor/tutor and Loam/Depths land-recursion inventories. These are actual event facts. Keep Dakmor/discard, Loam/recursion and compact threat packages; omit banned Primeval Titan. The specified Woodland/Analyst route provides a deterministic mana benchmark without asserting that random dredge triggers can always be shortcut to a win.

I2: Mark Domzil's1/59 and Philip Jorzik's1/21 Shorikai registrations supply a genuine legal Azorius artifact-control commander pedigree. Both are source registrations, not Stoneblade2HG results. Reto S.'s primary Stoneblade submission supplies Stoneforge/Batterskull/Kaldra with cantrips/counters; its page displays76 total cards and is not silently accepted as a valid60-card tournament list. The newly designed Shorikai Stoneblade hybrid keeps those defining threats, adds Pilot equipment carriers and removes source stax that hurts Gitrog.
