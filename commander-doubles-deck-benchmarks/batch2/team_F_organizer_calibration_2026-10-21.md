## Oracle / 2HG clarification
Lotho's wording is **"Whenever a player casts their second spell each turn"**. Your teammate is another player, so their second spell qualifies. Each player tracks their *own* spell count; second spell by player A and second spell by teammate B can each trigger Lotho in a shared turn. Treasure is made by **Lotho's controller only**, life loss comes out of the shared total. Verified via WotC LTR release notes and Oracle text (https://media.wizards.com/2023/downloads/LTR_Release_Notes/EN_MTGLTR_ReleaseNotes_20230508.pdf). Avoid wording implying each teammate receives Treasure.
