# Archetype ancestry and access limits

The factual foundation archive includes two complete100-card tournament registrations per head, with raw snapshot SHA rechecked, pilot locator, archived standing/field/record and dates. These results belong to the original ordinary Commander decks, never the new 2HG pairings. A registered pilot is not automatically the original designer. Some events are bracket-labelled; those labels are event declarations, not independent power verification. Source-card comparison is generated exactly rather than guessed.

D1 preserves the Chainer/Gary, ritual, reanimation and Vilis packages actually present in both selected registrations. The Michael Lehotsky registration also contains Citadel. Premium Mox/LED and other fast mana were cut under points; Necropotence was rejected on shared-turn grounds. K’rrik’s guaranteed CZ price is12, charged once as the alternative to its main8.

D2 registrations contain Ranger/Ranger-Captain, Ballista and Triskelion. The Modern Soul Sisters primary primer supplies the Warden/Attendant/Auriok Champion, protection and Heliod relationship, not a claim that a60-card Modern list can be copied into Commander. The singleton100 adds repeatable white card economy and team-aware support. Torpor Orb from a source list was rejected because it kills friendly Gary/Sister triggers. Spike Feeder is not imported into mono-white.

E1’s5th/24 source is an event explicitly labelled Bracket3; the other is14th/42. These modest results do not establish Nekusar as a top cEDH commander. We retain genuine wheels and punishments, but cut the source’s expensive Oracle/Breach and fast-artifact overlay to keep the approved strategic identity. The new Ghyrson/Prodigy 2HG damage plan is an adaptation, not a claimed tournament result.

E2’s Josh Cole registration includes Knowledge Pool and Force; Evan Hart supplies a different control/prison shell with Narset. Preserve these roles while replacing premium lands and extra paid free counters. Canonist/Omen Machine are specifically rejected for conflicts with allied wheels or own conversion. Scepter/Reversal provides a real independent close instead of expecting a lock alone to be a win.

F1’s Joseph Ward registration contains Salvagers/LED and generic Breya turbo packages; Khaalid Aysha’s contains Deathmantle. Neither is falsely described as the submitted Thopter-Sieve list. The artifact pivot is grounded separately in documented Modern Urza ThopterSword, existing Breya community lists and own Oracle/controller analysis. Keep Engineer/Welder/Deathmantle and artifact recursion, omit duplicate expensive Oracle/Breach, and buy Time Sieve/Orb control. Modern/prison pedigree describes engines, not proof of competitive2HG outcomes.

F2’s two selected Talion entries are both8th, in fields32 and27. Retain Oracle/Consultation, Remora, dense counters and enemy-cast card economy. Remove paid ancillary tutor/denial packages before reducing the broad defensive role. Do not add Null Rod/Cursed Totem merely because some Dimir source uses them: they would impair the ally’s artifact engine.
