# Conditional turn demonstrations

No games were played. These are fully specified resource sequences, not measured winning turns, opening-hand success probabilities or matchups. They assume the listed own cards/resources, required library contents and no successful opposition. Starting team skips its first draw step; no line borrows cards or mana from the other head.

Removal, graveyard exile, ETB suppression, life-gain denial and counters disrupt the line. No free two-head protection is smuggled into this seven.
