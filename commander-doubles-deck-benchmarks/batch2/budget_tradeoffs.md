# Budget and mana choices

The approved working cap is120 team,72 per deck and at most12 transferred from a60 reference. No requirement to spend points for its own sake. D72/48 transfers12; E56/64 transfers4; F57/63 transfers3. Each paid slot is reviewed below; full actual charges are appended by the generator.

D1 retains K’rrik CZ12, Tutor15, Citadel10, Dark Ritual9, Entomb8 and Reanimate6. They preserve deployment, unrestricted access and immediate conversion. Top3 and the one-point recursion/engine pieces remain efficient. Necropotence15 was replaced by Tutor15 on format grounds, not quietly discounted: shared draw-step skipping harms D2, and Necrodominance additionally blocks the graveyard. Peer5 was cut rather than exceeding72 or dropping essential recursion; Necrologia/Vilis/Citadel are conditional alternatives. Most other tutors are slower or conditional, not equivalent direct substitutes. Lake of the Dead, Coffers and Urborg remain baseline zero and are explicitly flagged for review. Mana from Lake needs real Swamp sacrifices.

D2 preserves Ranger-Captain13 for Ballista access plus opposing noncreature suppression, Enlightened9, Sentinel5, Drannith5 and Recruiter4. Heliod CZ2, Ballista2 and Felidar2 support independent finishes. Swords/Chant/Abeyance/Ascendant/Reservoir/Clamp cost1 each. Tithe8 and Grace4 were excluded to fund access/proactive play. Children1 was rejected for its false cross-player refund; there is no hidden refund for K’rrik payments. Sisters and white draw bodies are purposeful, not token filler. Starting60 makes Ascendant’s threshold immediately relevant; a flying blocker still blocks for either enemy.

E1 keeps Tutor15, Mystical7 and Gamble6 to find the exact engine/wheel; Gamble’s discard is real. Thief5 and paid wheel/punishment cards support distinct plans. Beseech6 was cut for BBB and price pressure; Muddle/Shred are limited two-mana access. E2 keeps Force14 with actual blue support, Teferi7 for team protection/Pool timing, Remora6 and Otawara6 as an ability-based exit, plus Drannith5 and Sentinel5. Lavinia CZ remains baseline1: feedback’s main-deck2 is not silently charged as a CZ alternative too. This scope interpretation is disclosed for review. Removing Canonist avoids sabotaging Scepter and allied wheels.

F1 preserves Sol Ring15 because recurring6 for Assembly and early generic deployment matter; Enlightened9 and Sieve8 are strategic anchors. Urza1, Transmute1 and free artifact-only tutors expose a price-family gap without changing the organizer’s sheet. Transmute is not a costless upgrade: sacrifice and any required mana-value difference still apply. F2 keeps Force14, Oracle10/Consultation10, Remora6, Otawara6 and Fluster5. Mystical7, Spellseeker4, Narset3 and Morphic Pool2 were cut; slower conditional access and an extra basic replace them. This is the actual16-point reduction from the79-point draft, preserving expensive channel utility.

## Land policy

All original10 fetches/original10 duals retain working5, shocks/surveils2, identified rainbow/special fixing1–2, Boseiju/Otawara6 within5–6. Batch2 has no green identity, so Boseiju is illegal in all six and is not used. Otawara is deliberately paid in E2/F2 and cut from E1/F1 under pressure.

There are no original duals or classic premium fetches in these six lists. We use basics and ordinary pain/fast/check/filter/Pathway/verge fixing rather than assigning imaginary discounts. Conditional fixing carries genuine sequencing requirements; filter lands cannot start colored mana alone, check/verge lands depend on other lands and late fastlands can enter tapped. E1’s BBB Dreams and F1’s WUBR require care; color identity does not prove mana production. E2 Prairie Stream/Mystic Sanctuary conditions use actual basic land types. Unpointed substitutes are a policy consequence to review, not a secret new land-pricing rule.

Malakir Rebirth is a known spell/land MDFC at inherited zero, not a newly approved zero. The organizer requested individual MDFC review. It appears in the approval queue with a one-point scenario: that would put D1 at73 until a documented substitution. Lunarch Veteran is transforming, not a spell/land MDFC; no blanket one-point tax is invented.
