# Commander Doubles — Season 0 v3 community review

**Start here: [View the 549-card points list](./POINTS_v3.csv)** · [Full research schedule (optional)](../candidate_point_schedule_v3.csv)

### The format, in 30 seconds
Competitive **2v2 Commander / Two-Headed Giant**: each team shares **60 life** and a **120-point deckbuilding budget** across two 100-card Commander decks. Normally each player gets 60 points, but teammates can transfer up to 12, making **72 the maximum for one player**. Existing Commander Doubles legality rules still apply.

### Reading the spreadsheet
- **In deck:** point cost if the card is anywhere in your 100-card deck other than the command zone. **In command zone:** special cost for a commander; a blank means the card has no applicable commander price in this sheet. Pay for each partner commander separately.
- **Cards absent from this sheet are 0 points** under this version (where legal), including deliberately unpointed cards such as **Acererak the Archlich**. Zero points does *not* establish format legality.
- The sheet includes **549 uniquely named point-bearing cards** (506 with positive main-deck prices and 76 positive command-zone prices; some overlap). Prices are whole numbers; the team total is the sum of all card point values in both decks.
- Examples: **Sol Ring 15**, **Demonic Tutor 15**, **Force of Will 14**, **Time Warp 12**, **Crop Rotation 6**, **Aluren 4**, **Stoneforge Mystic 2**, **Swan Song 2**. **Gitrog Monster 1 in deck / 6 in command zone**; **Shorikai 0 in deck / 4 in command zone**.

### What we're trying to accomplish
Prices reflect a card's strongest *realistically achievable* contribution to a deliberately optimized two-player team, balancing **efficiency** (speed/reliability), **conversion** (value into a win), **opportunity cost** (mana, setup, and dead cards), and **2HG-specific effects** (shared turns/life, two opponents, teammate interactions).

**Price versatile combo enablers more heavily than narrow, otherwise weak combo payoffs.** Thus Aluren costs 4 while Acererak can remain 0. Do not treat point totals as measured win probabilities.

### Feedback requested — three quick questions
1. **Too high or too low?** Name up to five cards and suggest a new price.
2. **Anything missing at 0?** Identify a broadly powerful card/combo enabler that deserves points.
3. **2HG mistakes?** Flag any shared-life, teammate, extra-turn, controller, or targeting interaction we overlooked.

**Ideal feedback:** `Card — current price → proposed price — one sentence why`. For commander prices say `command zone`.
