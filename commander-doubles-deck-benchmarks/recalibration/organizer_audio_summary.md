# Read-aloud organizer summary

We have now priced all eighteen original decks under one common organizer snapshot, then under a separate researched proposal. The decks are unchanged. The largest misses were efficient tutors, broad repeatable engines, and guaranteed commander access. We kept deliberate exceptions: Pox and Contamination are free, Acererak is free, and Aluren costs four. We did not double all commander prices or charge every combo component.

1. Intuition, in the main deck, goes from 1 to 8 proposed points. Three-mana instant selects three arbitrary names and routes two to own graveyard; opponent choice is a real constraint but often deliberately redundant.

2. Gifts Ungiven, in the main deck, goes from 1 to 8 proposed points. Four-mana instant distributes four different named cards between hand and graveyard; broad graveyard assembly is much more than a one-point payload.

3. Natural Order, in the main deck, goes from 1 to 8 proposed points. Four-mana sacrifice tutor cheats any green creature directly onto own battlefield; green/body restriction warrants less than unrestricted premium access.

4. Protean Hulk, in the main deck, goes from 1 to 8 proposed points. Death trigger finds an entire creature package of total mana value six; reusable assembly engine rather than ordinary interchangeable payoff, offset by seven-mana body/death requirement.

5. Grim Tutor, in the main deck, goes from 0 to 5 proposed points. Three-mana sorcery to any card in hand; slower than Demonic and costs three shared life, but broad consistency should not be free.

6. Final Parting, in the main deck, goes from 0 to 3 proposed points. Five mana puts one arbitrary card in hand and another in graveyard, enabling one-card assembly with substantially slower deployment.

7. Fabricate, in the main deck, goes from 0 to 4 proposed points. Any artifact to hand for three mana; broader artifact engines justify four but no direct battlefield cheating like Reshape.

8. Displacer Kitten, in the main deck, goes from 0 to 5 proposed points. Four-mana repeatable noncreature-spell blink engine resets own mana rocks, planeswalkers and tutors; does not blink teammate permanents.

9. Necrotic Ooze, in the main deck, goes from 1 to 4 proposed points. Four-mana creature combines activated abilities from all graveyards and converts many distinct payloads, while creature/graveyard hate remains effective.

10. Chainer, Dementia Master, in the main deck, goes from 0 to 3 proposed points. Five-mana repeatable reanimation engine from any graveyard using own BBB and three shared life; setup and exile-on-leave keep price modest.

11. Candelabra of Tawnos, in the main deck, goes from 0 to 4 proposed points. One-mana artifact repeatedly converts generic mana into land untaps, including teammate targets; tap cost and large-mana lands are genuine prerequisites.

12. Panoptic Mirror, in the main deck, goes from 1 to 6 proposed points. Reusable free instant/sorcery casts on own shared upkeep, including extra-team-turn loops; artifact5 plus imprint investment and exposure window reduce price.

13. Time Warp, in the main deck, goes from 6 to 12 proposed points. Five-mana one-card extra TEAM turn provides both players draw, mana and upkeep; no artifact-fodder prerequisite, counterable and target-dependent.

14. Malakir Rebirth // Malakir Mire, in the main deck, goes from 0 to 1 proposed points. Tapped-land fallback plus one-mana death protection can target ANY creature, including teammate's; return under owner control and two life paid by caster.

15. Urza, Lord High Artificer, in the command zone, goes from 3 to 8 proposed points. Guaranteed artifact-to-mana engine and mana sink offers broad repeatable conversion; tokens and mana belong only to its own controller.

The same extra turn gives both teammates a turn. That is why the three equivalent five-mana turn spells are proposed at twelve. Savor the Moment is lower because both friendly players skip untapping. Chainer, Ooze and Kitten are priced as reusable engines. The payload can still be free.
