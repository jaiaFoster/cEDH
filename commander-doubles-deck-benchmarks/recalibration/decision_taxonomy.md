# Organizer decision taxonomy and precedence

This pass reads all eleven B1–B3 reviews plus the premium-boundary ledger and consolidated166-row overlay. It preserves 231 effective protected card/location decisions, including explicit nonchanges and earlier unsuperseded decisions. 91 rows are classified as explicit numeric from original provenance; other rows retain working/approximate status. These counts are decisions, not measured card-strength observations.

The fifty historical0–5 anchors remain unchanged and are not numerical price bins. Later numeric decisions control only the named location. The full event chain and literal source mentions are in decision_lineage.csv; mentions may include superseded numbers and are not themselves the precedence algorithm. Broad family rows in the preview are assistant illustrations, not hundreds of organizer-approved exact prices.

## tutors

Access breadth, destination, timing and repeatability, not the word search alone.

Comparisons: Demonic Tutor 15; Enlightened 9; Call4; artifact tutors8–9; specialist Mages1.

Boundary: Expedition Map0 and Sylvan Scrying0 are intentional; not every search is charged.

## acceleration

Count net mana after real costs, setup, symmetry and ownership; no shared mana pools.

Comparisons: Dark Ritual9; Basalt4; Bloom Tender1; Lake6; Cradle15.

Boundary: One-mana ordinary dorks and ordinary ramp remain0; accepted Bloom Tender1 is not silently raised.

## lands

Land-slot opportunity cost and repeatable access matter, but not all nonbasics are premium.

Comparisons: Crop Rotation6; Boseiju/Otawara6; Shifting Woodland3; fetch/dual5 working.

Boundary: Stage, Depths, Loam, Exploration, Burgeoning and Map intentionally0; conditional/basic fixing mostly free.

## reusable_engines

Concentrate cost on portable repeated conversion; explicitly identify prerequisite ownership.

Comparisons: Aluren4; KCI10; Clock5; Mystic Forge6; Curio3.

Boundary: Acererak0, ordinary tokens and narrow borrowed abilities can be free; engine premium is not a pair surcharge.

## reanimation

Graveyard scope, mana cheated, reusable permissions and destination separate engine from payload.

Comparisons: Reanimate/Animate/Dance/Necromancy4 working; Replenish9 working.

Boundary: Ordinary expensive one-shot resurrection and narrow targets need no universal4-point family tax.

## interaction

Free protection and timing can defend a teammate, but targets and controller wording remain exact.

Comparisons: Force14; Fierce13; Silence14; Chant1; Abeyance1; Swan2; Shoal/Misdirection2.

Boundary: Counterspell, ordinary removal and intentionally free Cyclonic Rift are not mechanically taxed.

## mdfc

Comparisons: Sink into Stupor1.

## narrow_payoffs

Independent efficient utility may justify a small price; dead-outside-engine role alone does not.

Comparisons: Acererak0; Ballista2; Bloodthirsty3; Tenacity4.

Boundary: No automatic combo-membership or teammate premium; ordinary interchangeable payoffs may remain0.

## ordinary_or_prior

Document what was screened and inherited rather than pretending all prior tail ranks are independently measured.

Comparisons: Prior individual assessment retained pending specific contrary evidence.

Boundary: Most of the legal card pool stays free; a screen nomination is not a human approval.

No new coefficients were fitted. The four lenses guide explicit per-card judgments in proposals_v3.tsv: efficiency, resource conversion, opportunity cost and verified2HG delta. No point is added because a card appears frequently or merely participates in a combo. Price differences inside families are deliberate comparisons, not false precision from a regression.
