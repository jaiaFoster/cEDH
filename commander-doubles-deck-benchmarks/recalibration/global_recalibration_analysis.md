### Limits and sensitivity

The benchmark is eighteen selected, intentionally synergistic lists, not a random sample and not labelled game outcomes. The prices are human-calibrated policy judgments. A one-point change to a shared staple moves a team by up to2 because singleton is enforced independently per deck; a commander-only change moves only heads using that commander. Sparse cutoff families and explicitly free commanders create structural opportunities that a budget cannot fully predict. No inferential confidence interval is supplied for subjective prices; the included ±1/±2 scenario tables measure construction sensitivity only. Do not interpret their ranges as probabilistic confidence intervals.
