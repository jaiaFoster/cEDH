Commander Doubles is competitive 2v2 Commander using shared team turns and 60 shared starting life. Each player brings a 100-card Commander deck, including their commander or legal commander pair.

POINTS & BUDGET
Your team has 120 points. Each player has a nominal 60-point share and may receive up to 12 unused points from their teammate. No individual deck may exceed 72 points. Pay the main-deck price for cards in the main deck and the separate command-zone price for each commander. Known cards absent from the published point schedule cost zero, where legal. No automatic synergy surcharge applies.

DECK CONSTRUCTION
Use the existing Commander rules for color identity, singleton exceptions, command-zone eligibility, legal partners, and the applicable Commander ban list. Teammates may use the same card in their separate decks. Points compliance and construction legality are separate checks. The website uses the repository's pinned October 20, 2026 Oracle catalog; unknown identities require resolution and are never silently treated as free. Gameplay interactions and organizer policy still require judge review.

REGISTRATION & DECKS
Create one persistent team, optionally name both players, save two public Moxfield decks, and register for an open event. Anyone selecting a team may edit its unlocked submissions. Deck history is preserved so an organizer can restore accidental changes. Event decklists and prices freeze when locked or when the tournament begins. General team decks may still be edited for future events.

TOURNAMENTS
Challonge supplies brackets, pairings, match results, advancement, standings, and winners. Report results through Challonge. The public dashboard refreshes tournament information automatically and shows its last successful synchronization. All event times include a time zone; America/Phoenix is the default.

PRICE PROVENANCE
