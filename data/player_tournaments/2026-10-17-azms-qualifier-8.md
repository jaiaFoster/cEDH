## Round 2 — win
- Three-player pod; seat 1; opponents Thranduil and Vraska (exact versions unverified).
- Kept second seven: Machine God's Effigy; Mental Misstep; Silence; Imperial Seal; City of Traitors; "Buried Catacombs" [transcription uncertain]; Misty Rainforest.

- Won with infinite mana from Devoted Druid + Swift Reconfiguration (other conversion details not recorded).

## Round 3 — draw
- Three opponents: Kinnan to player's left, Tayam, Hashaton. Seat unconfirmed.
- Kept seven (first/second seven uncertain): Smothering Tithe; Mockingbird; Chrome Mox; Pact of Negation; Mental Misstep; Marsh Flats; Scrubland.
- Imprinted Mental Misstep under Chrome Mox.
- Kinnan developed a threatening early board. Hashaton player committed a board wipe at great cost; Kinnan countered it.
- Player used Pact of Negation to counter Kinnan's counterspell, with the expectation of dying to Pact upkeep if play continued.
- Player negotiated that the table draw if they used Pact; draw agreed. Game ended around turn two, ~10 minutes.
- Retrospective: Misstep on early Kinnan acceleration might have been an alternative, but would alter Chrome Mox imprint and Pact availability.

## Round 4 — loss
- Three-player pod; seat 3; turn order Blue Farm → Etali → player.
- Kept six: Rhystic Study; Finale of Devastation; Imperial Seal; Crop Rotation; Windswept Heath; Tropical Island. Delney bottomed.
- Blue Farm mulliganed to two (approximately Esper Sentinel and land).
- Etali had turn-one Etali and generated substantial value.
- Player used Imperial Seal to set up Sol Ring for planned turn-two Rhystic Study; Etali dashed Ragavan and exiled/stole that topdeck.
- Etali subsequently won through combat with an 11/11 infect threat (precise sequence unverified).
- Player and judge Eric discussed the keep as insufficiently proactive: no guaranteed T1 development, no interaction, conditional T2 Rhystic in a three-player pod.
- Retrospective: player believes mulliganing for meaningful early action would have been preferable.
