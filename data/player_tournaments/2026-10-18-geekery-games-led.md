## Cross-event synthesis (analysis, separate from observations)
1. **Pod-aware mulligans:** A T2 Tithe hand is not an automatic keep in a fast pod without an interaction or alternate early-development plan; repeats the AZMS #8 final-round concern.
2. **Conversion budget:** Identify the line's deterministic and nondeterministic requirements, preserve colored mana and interaction, and define when to stop and pivot to value.
3. **Politics:** Negotiate draws before paying an otherwise losing policing cost (Rounds 1 and 3); never rely on vague 'don't get a win' promises as substitute for checking actual tutor target and line.
4. **Engine access:** Protect Thrasios/outlets, Cradle creature count, and alternate lines, not merely mana volume.
5. **Avoid hindsight inflation:** G1/G3 draws were rational under reported conditions; G5 mulligan decision may be questionable but an alternative keep is not known to have prevented T2 Oracle.
