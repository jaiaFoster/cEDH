## Round 3 — Seat 3

**Pod/order as reported:** T&T to player's left; Derevi; Malcolm/Tana; Tymna-Thrasios.

**Mulligan:** Reported as a seven / possibly second seven (uncertain).

**Reported keep:** Mockingbird; Gaea's Cradle; Chrome Mox; Silence; Birthing Pod; Verdant Catacombs; Noble Hierarch; Birthing Pod was spoken twice, so one slot is uncertain.

**Development**
- Player broke parity early with Birthing Pod + Gaea's Cradle.
- Table became concerned about player's position.
- T&T stopped another player's win attempt that the player could not stop.
- In exchange, T&T leveraged a deal that effectively time-walked the player ("don't do anything").
- Player accepted because they expected to convert excess mana into Thrasios activations.

**Critical sequence**
- Derevi player copied player's Birthing Pod and activated the copy.
- Player did not know the Derevi list and passed rather than spending the available mana through Thrasios.
- Derevi found **Linvala**, shutting off activated abilities.
- The previously banked mana could no longer be converted through Thrasios.
- Player later had infinite green but no functioning activated outlet because of Linvala.
- Player had/could access Voice of Victory / Grand Abolisher protection during later attempts; Voice was removed with Deadly Rollick.
- Retrospective line: Gilded Drake on Linvala would both remove the lock and restore Thrasios access. Player believed resources/Pod activations may have existed to construct a protected version of this line.

**Endgame**
- Malcolm player put a win on the stack.
- T&T player recurred Deadly Rollick and then negotiated/offered a draw around the stop.

**Result:** **Draw.**

**Retrospective:** This was identified as a major missed conversion window. Key lesson: banked potential is not realized value; if an unknown tutor/Pod activation can invalidate the planned mana outlet, consider cashing Thrasios activations before allowing it to resolve.

**Other possible interaction**

**Result:** **Loss.**

**Retrospective**

- The table allowed Arcum and Sisay to remain simultaneous engines too long; player could not efficiently police both alone.

## Event-level observations

These are retrospective hypotheses, not raw facts:

1. **Conversion timing remains the largest recurring leak.** Round 3 is a particularly clean example: Pod + Cradle created the advantage, but the player accepted delay and an opponent's Linvala converted unrealized Thrasios value into unusable mana.
2. **Do not donate the table's interaction bill.** Round 2 showed that stopping the first win can simply hand the game to the untouched player if the interaction leaves the player depleted.
3. **Prefer action-based deals over outcome-based deals.** "Do not activate Sisay" is objectively verifiable; "do not get something that lets you win" depends on threat knowledge and interpretation.
4. **Cash resources before unknown effects can invalidate the outlet.** An opponent's tutor/Pod activation can justify activating Thrasios before resolution when the mana otherwise disappears as usable equity.
5. **Avoid being sole police for two live engines.** Push other players with available interaction to answer one threat while it is cheap to do so.
