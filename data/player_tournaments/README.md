# Player Tournament Archive

Persistent first-party tournament records for the Tymna the Weaver / Thrasios, Triton Hero project.

These records are reconstructed from the player's post-event notes and conversation logs so deck-building, piloting, metagame, and primer agents can reference the same historical evidence.

## Evidence convention

- **Recorded**: directly reported by the player.
- **Reconstructed**: organized from the player's narrative; wording may be normalized.
- **Uncertain**: a card/deck/event detail was unclear or voice transcription may be garbled. Do not silently "correct" these fields.
- Analysis is kept separate from the raw match record where practical.
- Do not treat inferred dates, unknown cards, or retrospective counterfactuals as observed facts.

## Suggested fields for future events

For each round, preserve: table/seat, opposing commanders, mulligan/keep, kept hand (or photo reference), first engine, first major fight and personal interaction spent, parity-break point, first credible win window vs actual attempt, result/reason, consequential decision, and optional MVP/dead-card observations.
