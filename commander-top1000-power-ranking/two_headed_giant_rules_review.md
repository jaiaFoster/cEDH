# Two-Headed Giant rules review

Reviewed on 2026-10-19 against the [September 25, 2026 Comprehensive Rules](https://media.wizards.com/2026/downloads/MagicCompRules%2020260925.txt), the [official format page](https://magic.wizards.com/en/formats/two-headed-giant), current Oracle text and [banned list](https://magic.wizards.com/en/banned-restricted-list). `data/rules_source.json` records the retrieved text checksum. This is a rules-informed construction experiment, not a gameplay rules engine.

| Issue | Verified consequence and scoring implication |
| --- | --- |
| Starting life | Official Commander 2HG guidance gives 60 shared life. Players retain separate hands, mana and permanents. |
| Team result | CR810.8a makes a player's win/loss and cannot-win/cannot-lose effects apply to their team. Empty-library loss, delayed Pact loss and alternate wins can decide the team result. |
| Life events | CR810.9 applies damage, life loss and life gain to individuals before updating shared life. An effect referring to **your** life-loss event does not automatically trigger from the teammate's event. Costs use the shared total, but teammates cannot supply each other's mana. |
| Each opponent/player | Both opposing heads are affected by each-opponent life loss. Each-player effects also affect both friendly heads. Effects setting every player's life total use one chosen member per team (CR810.9d), rather than summing two independently set totals. |
| Commander damage | CR903.10a/704.6c track 21 combat damage from a commander to an individual player. The format webpage says per team, a documentation conflict. Prefer the Comprehensive Rules and track heads separately; obtain a league ruling if choosing a different policy. |
| Poison | A team loses at 15 poison. One proliferate action adds at most one poison counter to a team, even if both heads are chosen (CR701.34b). Blightsteel's 11 infect damage alone is not the team poison threshold. |
| Shared turns | CR805.8 makes extra turns and skipped steps teamwide. Necropotence and Necrodominance skip both normal draw steps. Seedborn Muse/Prophet ordinarily provide one opposing-team untap window, rather than three opponent turns. |
| Spell histories | Individual players' spell counts remain separate. Kraum, Mindbreak Trap, Lotho and similar effects do not simply combine teammates' spells. Vivi and Crystal trigger only from their controller's spells. |
| Combat | Shared defense does not merge creature control. Tymna can count teammate-inflicted combat damage to an opponent. Edric benefits creatures controlled by both teammates hitting opposing heads; enemy attacks do not hit Edric's opponents and do not trigger it in two-team play. |
| Protection | The One Ring protects its controller, not the teammate or shared life total. Teferi's Protection locks shared life and phases only its controller's permanents; poison, commander damage and explicit loss still matter. Angel's Grace instead supplies team cannot-lose protection for that turn, including those loss routes, but does not permit unaffordable life payments. |
| Life conversion | K'rrik replaces own black symbols, not generic costs, commander tax or teammate payments. Its mana value remains seven. Ad Nauseam adds cards without drawing and causes life loss rather than payment; resolution can continue below zero, followed by a state-based loss unless protected. Ancient Tomb and Fastbond cause damage, not life-payment costs. |
| Team-turn control | Mindslaver controls the opposing team turn. Two friendly Time votes for Expropriate can produce two extra team turns. These are separate legality-review concerns, not team-synergy budget premiums. |
| Symmetrical life effects | At 60, Heartless Hidetsugu deals 30 to each head, normally killing both teams simultaneously. At 59, it deals 29 to each head, leaving one shared life. Prevention or asymmetric protection must be independently established. Sorin targeting one head sets the shared total to ten; Biorhythm uses the chosen-member life-setting rule. |
| Eligibility | CR903.3 permits legendary creatures, legendary Vehicles and legendary Spacecraft with qualifying power/toughness boxes, plus explicit commander permissions and Grist's off-battlefield creature effect. Use DFC front-face eligibility. Backgrounds require a compatible Choose a Background; partner families must match. Doctor's companion excludes a changeling with additional creature types. |
| Legality | Commander bans remain bans regardless of hypothetical prices. Erayo has an additional Constructed 2HG ban. Lutri is currently Commander-legal as a card/commander but remains banned as companion. |

Card-specific numerical adjustments remain provisional judgments. No cross-controller combo is certified by a union of card names. The preserved Spellbook prototype should enumerate prerequisites and controller assumptions for a separate league ruling.
